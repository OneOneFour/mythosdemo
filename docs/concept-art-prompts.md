# Concept Art Prompt Pack — *Vertical Alchemy* (working title)

For Nano Banana / Nano Banana Pro. Written as descriptive prose rather than tag soup, which is what these models respond to best.

---

## How to use this

**Build a consistent set, don't roll dice.** Generate Prompt 0 (the style plate) first. Once you get one you like, feed that image back in as a reference on every subsequent prompt with a line like: *"Match the art style, palette, and line quality of the attached reference image exactly."* This is the single highest-leverage thing you can do — without it you'll get eleven unrelated illustrations.

**Aspect ratios matter a lot here.** A vertical game needs tall frames. Ask explicitly: "Aspect ratio 9:16" or "2:5, extremely tall vertical composition." Models default to landscape and will crop your world flat if you let them.

**The hard part is the cross-section.** These models want to give you a dramatic 3/4 perspective cave painting. You have to fight for the flat side-on cutaway. Every prompt below contains an explicit clause for this — keep it verbatim, and if a generation drifts into perspective, add: *"Strictly flat side elevation. No perspective, no vanishing point, no foreshortening. The cut face of the earth is parallel to the picture plane, like an architectural section drawing."*

**Iterate by editing, not rerolling.** Once a composition is right, use follow-up edit instructions ("add a second elevator shaft on the left third", "make the lava glow warmer") rather than regenerating from scratch.

## Prompt 0 — Style plate (generate this first)

```
Concept art style reference plate for a 2D side-scrolling underground factory game with a Greek mythology and alchemy theme. Painterly digital illustration with visible brush texture, hand-inked dark outlines on machinery, and soft atmospheric washes on rock and air. Palette: warm terracotta, bronze, and oxidised copper-green for all human-made machinery; cool slate blue-grey and violet for stone strata; hot amber and blood-orange for fire and molten material; pale bone-white for divine light. Muted, earthy, slightly grimy overall — the feeling of soot, ash, and old metal, not clean sci-fi. Composition is a flat side-on cutaway cross-section of the earth, as if the ground has been sliced vertically with a knife and we are looking straight at the cut face. Strictly flat side elevation, no perspective and no vanishing point, like an architectural section drawing rendered as painted illustration. Show a small sample of machinery: a stone alchemical vat, a wooden chute, a clay pipe, a hanging chain, and a rough-hewn tunnel through banded rock. Aspect ratio 16:9.
```

## Prompt 1 — Key art: the full vertical world

The poster shot. Everything at once, top to bottom.

```
Extremely tall vertical key art for a mythological underground factory game. A single continuous flat side-on cutaway cross-section of the entire world from the heavens down to hell, sliced open so we see every layer at once in one unbroken column. At the very top, blinding bone-white cloud and the marble colonnades of the sky gods, tiny and unreachable. Below that, a thin strip of green sunlit surface with olive trees and a small dig site. Then descending bands of rock, each layer darker and stranger than the last: pale limestone with clay pipes, banded ochre strata threaded with copper veins and wooden chutes, a flooded blue aquifer level with waterfalls pouring into the dark, a deep red zone of lava channels and glowing brine, and finally at the very bottom a black abyss with faint bioluminescent eyes and vast bone architecture. Connecting all of it, a chaotic tangle of chains, elevator shafts, chutes, and pipes running the full height of the image like a spine — dense, jury-rigged, organic. A single tiny human figure with a lantern is visible mid-depth for scale, dwarfed by the machinery. Strictly flat side elevation, no perspective, no vanishing point, the cut face of the earth parallel to the picture plane. Painterly digital illustration, warm bronze machinery against cool stone, dramatic vertical light gradient from divine white at the top to absolute black at the bottom. Aspect ratio 9:16, extremely tall composition.
```

## Prompt 2 — Act 1: the surface dig site

Hour one. Deliberately pathetic.

```
Early-game concept art for a mythological underground factory game. Flat side-on cutaway cross-section of a small, humble surface dig site in ancient Greece: a sun-bleached hillside with two olive trees and dry golden grass on top, and beneath it a single crude vertical shaft dug perhaps twenty feet down into pale limestone. The setup is deliberately primitive and inadequate — one hand-cut wooden chute carrying rocks downward, a hand-cranked windlass with a frayed rope, a clay pot over a small wood fire, and a pile of loose rubble. A lone barefoot figure in a torn grey chiton swings a pickaxe at the shaft floor by lantern light. Warm afternoon sunlight floods the top third of the image; the bottom of the shaft is already in deep shadow. Unexplored darkness presses in at the edges of the excavation, hinting at ore veins just outside the dug space. Strictly flat side elevation, no perspective, no vanishing point, like an architectural section drawing. Painterly digital illustration, muted earthy palette, dusty and sunlit and lonely. Aspect ratio 3:4.
```

## Prompt 3 — Act 2: first alchemy and the aquifer

```
Mid-early-game concept art for a mythological underground factory game. Flat side-on cutaway cross-section showing four excavated levels connected by wooden chutes and a single dripping water channel. On the surface level, automated bronze axe-arms fell a small olive grove and timber tumbles into a dedicated vertical fuel shaft. Two levels down, a cluster of squat stone alchemical vats bubble with luminous green and violet fluid, tended by clay pipes and stirred by chain-driven paddles. To one side, the player has breached a shallow underground aquifer: clear pale-blue water spills out of the rock wall in a small waterfall, is caught in a carved stone basin, and is routed by clay pipe toward the vats as coolant. Steam curls where the cold water meets a hot pot. Everything is slightly wrong and improvised — mismatched pipe diameters, a chute propped up with rocks, a leak patched with rags. Strictly flat side elevation, no perspective, no vanishing point, cut face of the earth parallel to the picture plane. Painterly digital illustration, terracotta and copper machinery against pale limestone, lit by firelight from the vats and cold blue glow from the water. Aspect ratio 3:4.
```

## Prompt 4 — Act 3: mid-depth spaghetti

The "embrace the spaghetti" shot. This is the one that sells the game.

```
Mid-game concept art for a mythological underground factory game, showing a gloriously chaotic factory. Flat side-on cutaway cross-section of roughly eight excavated levels carved through banded ochre and grey rock, absolutely crammed with overlapping industrial alchemy. Dozens of wooden and bronze chutes cross and bypass each other at every angle, carrying ore downward. Clay and bronze pipes weave through natural cave voids, some following the rock, some hacked straight through it. Three separate elevator shafts run upward, each a column of chain, counterweights, and roaring furnace-driven burner platforms belching black smoke into vent holes. Stone vats, distillers with copper coils, and screw presses are wedged into every available pocket of space. Minecarts on rickety timber rails. Steam vents crossing lava channels. Nothing is aligned to a grid; everything conforms to the shape of the discovered cave system. It should look organic, jury-rigged, and barely holding together, like a Piranesi engraving of an alchemical refinery. A tiny figure on a rope ladder is nearly lost in the machinery. Strictly flat side elevation, no perspective, no vanishing point, like an architectural section drawing. Painterly digital illustration, dense detail, warm bronze and soot-black against cool stone, lit in pools by scattered furnace fires. Aspect ratio 4:5.
```

## Prompt 5 — Act 4: the thermal zone

```
Late-game concept art for a mythological underground factory game. Flat side-on cutaway cross-section of a deep, dangerous, superheated layer of the earth. The rock is dark red-black basalt, cracked and glowing along its seams. A wide channel of molten lava runs horizontally through the frame, tapped by stone sluice gates that divert glowing orange flow into a bank of alchemical crucibles. Directly above, precariously, sits a rank of cooling towers fed by a pale blue-white slush pipeline descending from far above — the two systems inches apart, the air between them shimmering and distorted with heat haze. Hot mineral brine, sulphurous yellow-green, pools in carved basins and is pumped through insulated clay pipe wrapped in rags. Everything metal in this zone is warped, discoloured, and stress-cracked. Thick smoke and steam layer near the ceiling of every chamber. A single figure in heavy heat-shielding wrappings stands silhouetted against the lava glow. Strictly flat side elevation, no perspective, no vanishing point, cut face of the earth parallel to the picture plane. Painterly digital illustration, extreme contrast between hot orange-red and cold blue-white, everything else in near-black silhouette. Aspect ratio 4:5.
```

## Prompt 6 — Act 5: the Abyss

```
Endgame concept art for a mythological underground factory game, depicting the Abyss. Flat side-on cutaway cross-section of the deepest layer of the world, where geology gives way to something alive and wrong. The stone is black and glassy, veined with faintly pulsing violet light. Vast ribs and vertebrae of some enormous dead titan form natural archways through the rock. Bioluminescent fungal growths in sickly teal cling to the ceilings. Pale, eyeless, many-limbed creatures nest in the corners of chambers, and one has torn open a bronze pipe and is drinking from it. The player's factory has pushed down into this space and looks fragile and out of place here: a lone reinforced elevator shaft, a harvesting rig draining glowing golden ichor from a slain beast into stoppered amphorae, spike barricades, and guttering torches that barely hold back the dark. Below the lowest chamber, the cross-section fades into absolute featureless black with two distant points of light that might be eyes. Strictly flat side elevation, no perspective, no vanishing point, like an architectural section drawing. Painterly digital illustration, near-monochrome black and violet with piercing accents of golden ichor and orange torchlight, oppressive and claustrophobic. Aspect ratio 4:5.
```

## Prompt 7 — The demand side: the tribute altar

Cut back to the top to show who you're doing all this for.

```
Concept art for the tribute delivery point in a mythological factory game. Flat side-on cutaway cross-section of the topmost level of the world, where the player's grimy industrial elevator shaft finally breaks the surface and meets the divine. The bottom two-thirds of the frame is soot-stained bronze machinery: the head of a burner elevator, chains, gears, smoke, and a wooden loading platform stacked with crates of finished goods — gleaming divine plates, stoppered bottles of glowing ambrosia, small perfect ingots. The top third is overwhelming bone-white marble and blinding cloud, utterly clean, in which floats a colossal serene marble hand, palm up and open, waiting. The scale contrast is absurd: the hand is larger than the entire elevator head. Between them, a stone altar where offerings are placed, and a hanging tablet listing quotas in incised Greek lettering. Nothing divine is dirty; nothing mortal is clean. Strictly flat side elevation, no perspective, no vanishing point, cut face parallel to the picture plane. Painterly digital illustration, harsh value split between blown-out white above and warm grimy bronze below. Aspect ratio 3:4.
```

## Prompt 8 — God boon cards (UI asset)

Swap the bracketed machine each time. Use Nano Banana Pro if you want the text legible.

```
Single-object concept art for a reward card in a mythological factory game. Centred three-quarter view of one piece of ancient alchemical machinery, isolated on a flat dark charcoal background with a subtle radial glow behind it. The object is [A DIVINE COUNTERWEIGHT LIFTER: a tall bronze frame holding a marble sphere on a chain, wrapped in olive branches, humming with pale gold light]. Constructed from cast bronze, rough-hewn stone, olive wood, and rope, with Greek key patterns and incised lettering as decoration. Weathered and patinated, clearly ancient, but radiating a faint supernatural light that ordinary machines in this world do not have. Dramatic single light source from the upper left, strong rim light, soft ambient occlusion. Painterly digital illustration with clean silhouette suitable for use as a game UI card. No background scenery, no ground plane, no text. Aspect ratio 3:4.
```

## Prompt 9 — Bestiary sheet

```
Character design sheet for enemies in a Greek mythology underground factory game. Five creature designs arranged in a row on a plain warm-grey paper background, each shown in flat side profile as they would appear in a 2D side-scrolling game. The creatures are subterranean, pale, and drawn from Greek chthonic myth reimagined as cave fauna: a blind burrowing hound with fused eyelids and copper-stained claws; a swarm of finger-length bronze-shelled scarabs that eat metal; a tall thin humanoid of wet grey clay with an unformed face; a serpentine thing with a ring of stone-grinding teeth; and a large armoured beast with a cracked shell that glows golden at the seams where ichor leaks out. All designs emphasise silhouette readability at small size. Consistent scale reference line beneath each. Painterly digital illustration, desaturated palette of bone-white, wet grey, and chitinous black with sparing golden ichor accents. Flat side profile, no perspective. Aspect ratio 16:9.
```

## Prompt 10 — Failure state: the flood

Worth having early. It communicates stakes better than any amount of factory porn.

```
Dramatic concept art of a disaster in a mythological underground factory game. Flat side-on cutaway cross-section of a deep multi-level factory being destroyed by flooding. The player has breached a subterranean lake: a torrent of dark blue-green water blasts horizontally out of a ruptured rock wall and the lowest three levels are already fully submerged, machinery drowned and visible as ghostly shapes beneath the surface. The waterline is rising toward a mid-level furnace bank, where water meeting fire throws up a violent wall of white steam. Chutes and pipes hang snapped and dangling. Crates of finished goods float and tumble in the current. Near the top of the frame, a tiny figure scrambles up a swaying rope ladder toward the light, one level ahead of the water. Strictly flat side elevation, no perspective, no vanishing point, like an architectural section drawing. Painterly digital illustration, cold drowning blue-green flooding upward into warm firelit bronze, high contrast, chaotic and kinetic. Aspect ratio 9:16, tall vertical composition.
```

## Prompt 11 — Protagonist design

```
Character design sheet for the protagonist of a Greek mythology underground factory game: a Prometheus-like figure, condemned and unbreakable. Three views on a plain warm-grey background — front, side profile, and back. A lean, weathered, ageless human figure in a torn and soot-blackened grey chiton, barefoot, forearms and shins wrapped in scorched linen. Heavy bronze manacles remain locked on both wrists with the chains snapped short, a permanent mark of the sentence. Long dark hair tied back roughly. A deep, badly healed vertical scar across the ribs on the left side. Carries a soot-stained pickaxe and a small hooded oil lantern. Expression tired, patient, and entirely unafraid. Not heroic, not muscular, not idealised — a labourer serving an eternal sentence. Painterly digital illustration, muted earthy palette of ash grey, dust, and dull bronze. Include a small separate detail study of the manacles and the lantern. Aspect ratio 16:9.
```

## If you only generate three

Prompt 1 (key art), Prompt 4 (spaghetti), Prompt 7 (tribute altar). Those three together communicate the entire premise — the vertical world, the chaos it produces, and the reason you're suffering — which is what you need for a pitch deck or a Steam page test.
