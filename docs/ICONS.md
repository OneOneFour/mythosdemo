# Icon reference

Every resource, craftable, machine and god-gift in the game, enumerated from
the data tables. Written for someone drawing pixel art who has not read the
code.

Every row here was generated by importing `src/data/*.js` and
`src/model/items.js` and printing what the real predicates return. Nothing is
hand-listed. If a pair is absent from section 2, the code says it cannot
exist.

---

## 1. Why there is no flat item list

An item in this game is two rows multiplied, never one. A **substance** is an
element — copper, tin, timber, stone, adamant. A **form** is a shape an element
can be held in — ore, gravel, ingot, plate, log, brand, rung, block. Anything
you can hold is a `{substance, form}` pair, and its name, mass, size and colour
are computed from both rows (`src/data/forms.js:547` `labelOf`,
`src/model/items.js:42` `massOf`).

So "copper ore" and "tin ore" are one form crossed with two substances. There
is one `ore` row, one `smelt` recipe and one `ingot` row, and they cover every
metal that will ever be added. Drawing an icon per pair is therefore drawing
**substance colour × form silhouette**, and a shared silhouette for a form is
worth more than 44 unique drawings.

Not every crossing is legal. A form declares `subTags` — which substance tags
may take it — and `crossable()` (`src/data/forms.js:385`) is the whole rule.
`ingot` requires `metal`, so there is no stone ingot. `phial` requires
`miracle`, so a miracle can never satisfy a trinket selector. 27 substances ×
13 forms is 351 combinations; **44 are legal and holdable.**

A tile in the world stores one byte, which packs a substance ordinal and a form
ordinal together (`src/data/forms.js:476`). That is the price of this model and
it caps how many substances can ever be terrain.

## 2. Holdable pairs — 44, grouped by form

Columns:

- **mass (T)** — talents, `SUB[sub].item.mass × FORM[form].massK`
  (`src/model/items.js:44`).
- **hard (s)** — seconds to break when placed as a tile at pick power 1,
  `SUB.tile.hard × FORM.tile.hardK` (`src/model/tiles.js:119`). `—` means the
  form is not tile-capable and never renders as terrain.
- **tier** — the pick tier needed to mine the *native* substance
  (`SUB.tile.tier`, absent = 1). `—` means the substance is not terrain.
- **obtained** — the path that actually exists in the shipped content.
- **sprite** — a dedicated shape in `src/view/sprites.js`, with its draw size.

## 3. Tile-capable forms — terrain, not icons

Four of the thirteen forms carry a `tile` block. A placed unit of one becomes a
byte in the tile grid and is painted by the chunk renderer, so its art is
**tile art at the band's 8 px tile size**, not an icon. They still need an
*item* icon for the pocket strip, because you carry them before you place them.

| form | `tile` block | what it is | own `look`? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rung` (`src/data/forms.js:228`) | `solid:false, climb:true, hardK:0.20` | tier-1 timber ladder peg | yes — `ladder` treatment, 1 px rails inset 1 px in `woodC`, a `woodA` rung every 3rd band row |
| `stair` (`src/data/forms.js:267`) | `solid:false, climb:true` | tier-2 bronze stair, `climbK:1.8` | yes — same `ladder` function, rails on the tile edge, 2 px tread every 4th band row, copper tones |
| `block` (`src/data/forms.js:322`) | `solid:true, climb:false, hardK:1.0` | packed earth, the way back to solid ground | no — painted as generic terrain in the substance's colours |
| `seed` (`src/data/forms.js:369`) | `solid:false, climb:false, hardK:0.05, roots:true` | a planted seedling that becomes a tree | no |

A form with a `look` block **replaces the whole generic cube pass** — base
fill, grain, lit top face, cliff faces, bottom shade and the substance's own
treatments are all skipped (`src/data/forms.js:54`). So `rung` and `stair` draw
themselves and nothing else; `block` and `seed` read as ordinary rock.

**`stair` always draws in copper tones.** A form `look` cannot see which
substance it was crossed with, so a `tin/stair` would render copper. Not
reachable today, and recorded in `docs/FINDINGS.md`.

**CLAUDE.md D12 — a form is either feedstock or buildable, never both.** A form
carrying a `tile` block may not be named by any recipe's `in:`, any machine's
`handFeed.from`, or any tribute demand. `gravel` and `log` both used to be
placeable and both lost their `tile` block for it. For the artist this means the
placeable set is small and closed: **`rung`, `stair`, `block`, `seed`, plus
native terrain.** Rubble and logs are never walls.

## 5. Recipes — the refinement chain

19 rows in `src/data/recipes.js`, all of them `hand:true`. A `<selector>`
output means the substance is **derived** from whichever input satisfied the
clause, so one row covers every element.

### The chain

```
copper/tin wall --mine-->  ore  --smelt(4:1)-->  ingot  --press(3:1)-->  plate
timber trunk    --mine-->  log  --kindle(1:3)--> brand
                           log  --peg_rungs(2:4)-->  rung  (placeable ladder)
rock/soil wall  --mine-->  gravel --pack(5:1)-->  block  (placeable wall)
plate + log     --daedalan(2+4:2)-->  copper/stair  (placeable, faster ladder)
plate + log     --auger(2+1:1)-->  auger/relic      (tier-2 pick)
```

12 ore → 1 plate is the locked compression ratio (`docs/SPEC.md` §8).

## 8. Hard constraints

Quoted from `CLAUDE.md` and measured from the code.

### Pixels

- **Integer pixels only. No `fillText`.** (`CLAUDE.md` invariant 11.) Every
  draw goes through `R()` or `lineTo()` in `src/core/pixels.js`. There is no
  `clip()` and no alpha-blended edge to hide behind.
- **No sub-pixel positioning, no antialiased text.** A sprite that needs a
  half-pixel does not fit the renderer.
- **The HUD is drawn in the same pixel space as the world**, with the 5×7
  bitmap font in `src/core/font.js`. Glyphs are 5 columns × 7 rows; the
  descender row is dropped. Icon art must sit next to 7 px-tall capitals.

### Resolution and upscale

- Base buffer defaults to **320 × 180 at scale 3** (`src/core/canvas.js:15`).
- `resize()` recomputes all three — `scale = clamp(round(windowHeight / 400),
  2, 6)`, `w = max(200, ceil(windowWidth / scale))` and
  `h = max(180, ceil(windowHeight / scale))` (`src/core/canvas.js:38`). So the
  upscale factor runs **2× to 6×**, nearest-neighbour, applied by CSS.
- **200 px is the narrowest base width any widget must stay legible at**
  (`BASE_W_MIN`, `src/core/canvas.js:33`).
- A **tile is 8 px** in all three bands (`src/data/world.js:54,68,147`).

### Sizes to draw at

| thing | size | where |
|---|---|---|
| generic dropped item | `FORM.size` — 2 px (`seed`), 3 px (`gravel`, `brand`, `phial`, `rung`), 4 px (everything else) | `src/model/items.js:43` |
| sprite-backed dropped item | the sprite's own `size`, overriding the form's — 9 px and 10 px today | `src/view/paint.js:558` |
| a placed tile | 8 px square | `src/data/world.js` |
| inventory / quickbar slot | 16 px cell, 1 px gap, 2 px pad → **12 × 12 usable** | `src/view/ui/slot.js:20,42` |
| a machine | `tw × th` tiles × 8 px — 8×8 px (`gear`), 24×16 px (`furnace`), 32×8 px (`belt_r`), 8×24 px (`cyclops_maw`) | `src/data/machines.js` |

The pickup hitbox and the drawn size are independent. A relic's hitbox is the
form's 4 px; its sprite draws at 10 px. Draw for legibility, not for the hitbox.

### Palette

- **Every colour must be a named entry in `src/core/palette.js`**, reached by
  name through `src/data/palette.js#colour`. `colour()` throws on an unknown
  name, and `tools/content.mjs` checks every name in a `look` block at build
  time.
- **Add named entries rather than inlining hex** (`CLAUDE.md`). Art-direction
  aliases go in `src/data/palette.js`; new hex goes in `src/core/palette.js`.
- **92 names exist today**:

```

grassA grassB grassC  soil soilA soilC
limeA limeB limeC limeD  ochreA ochreB ochreC ochreD  clayA clayB clayC

basA basB basC basD  lavaA lavaB lavaC lavaD  abyA abyB abyC
vio vioHi  bone boneD  hadA hadB hadC  shade

```

The suffix is the tone. `A` is the lightest and `D` the darkest. `ichor` is the
divine gold used for "special, look here" and nothing else.

### Determinism

- **No `rand()` in a draw path.** `CLAUDE.md` invariant 7. Animation is derived
  from the clock `t` the caller passes in, or from a position hash. All three
  existing sprites bob with `Math.sin(t * 2.4)`.
- **Screenshots are bit-exact.** `npm run test:visual` compares at
  `threshold: 0` and `maxDiffPixels: 0`. Any pixel that moves fails the suite.
  A deliberate change is accepted with `npm run test:visual:update` and the
  reason stated in the commit.

### No new machinery

- **No runtime dependencies.** No image loader, no sprite sheet format, no
  bundler. A sprite is a function that calls `R()` and `lineTo()`.
- **No substance or machine name may appear anywhere in `src/view/`.** Art is
  reached by name from a data row — `look.sprite` into
  `src/view/sprites.js#SPRITE`, `look.treatments[].fn` into
  `src/view/treatments.js#TREAT`, and `look.parts[].fn` into that same `TREAT`
  table (`src/view/paint.js:599`). Adding
  art is adding a generic entry plus a name on a content row, never a branch on
  an id.
